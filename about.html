About REKISTIO

Welcome to REKISTIO, a lightweight utility web tool designed to help users quickly verify, inspect, resize, and optimize photos against strict document specifications.

Our Mission

Millions of online application forms for passports, visas, entrance exams, and government job portals reject image submissions due to minor technical mismatches—such as incorrect pixel dimensions, aspect ratio errors, or file sizes exceeding strict limits (e.g., max 50 KB).

REKISTIO was built to eliminate this frustration by offering an instant, accurate, and completely free photo requirement checker that works in any web browser.

Why Choose REKISTIO?

  • 100% Privacy Focused: Photos are processed entirely on your device. We never store or view your files.
  • Instant Auto-Fix: Automatically crops, resizes, and compresses photos to meet your required dimensions and target KB limits.
  • No Software Needed: Works directly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop browsers without installation.
